# AquaFlow

## Project Description

AquaFlow is a Water Order and Delivery System that allows customers to conveniently order water, add products to their cart, schedule a preferred delivery date, and choose a payment method.

## Purpose

The purpose of AquaFlow is to provide a simple and organized way of managing water orders and deliveries. It helps customers place orders conveniently while allowing the system to manage delivery schedules and payment options.

## Features

- Water product browsing
- Add to cart
- Order management
- Delivery date scheduling
- GCash payment
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Order confirmation
- Delivery management

## Usage
1. Open the AquaFlow system.
2. Browse the available water products.
3. Select the desired quantity and add the product to the cart.
4. Review the items in the cart.
5. Select a preferred delivery date.
6. Choose GCash or Cash on Delivery as the payment method.
7. Confirm the order.
8. The order will be processed according to the selected delivery schedule.

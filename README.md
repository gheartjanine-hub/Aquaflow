# AquaFlow – Water Order & Delivery System

AquaFlow is a Water Order and Delivery System designed to make ordering and scheduling water deliveries easier and more convenient. Users can browse available water products, add their selected items to a cart, choose a preferred delivery date, and select their preferred payment method.

## Overview

AquaFlow provides a simple platform where customers can manage their water orders without needing to place orders manually. The system organizes the ordering, scheduling, and payment process in one place.

Customers can select their desired water products, specify the quantity, add them to their cart, schedule a delivery date, and choose between GCash or Cash on Delivery (COD) as their payment method.

## Features

- Add to Cart – Customers can add water products and specify quantities before placing an order.
- Water Product Ordering – Browse and select available water products.
- Delivery Scheduling – Customers can choose their preferred date for delivery.
- GCash Payment – Allows customers to pay through GCash.
- Cash on Delivery – Customers can choose COD and pay upon receiving their order.
- Order Management – Customers can review their selected products, quantities, and total amount before confirming an order.
- Delivery Processing – Orders can be organized according to their scheduled delivery dates.
- Customer Management – Stores customer information needed for order and delivery processing.

## Ordering Process

1. The customer browses the available water products.
2. The customer selects the desired product and quantity.
3. The selected items are added to the cart.
4. The customer reviews the order and total amount.
5. The customer chooses a preferred delivery date.
6. The customer selects a payment method:
   - GCash
   - Cash on Delivery (COD)
7. The customer confirms the order.
8. The order is processed and scheduled for delivery.

## Payment Methods

### GCash

Customers can choose GCash as their payment option and complete the payment before the order is processed.

### Cash on Delivery

Customers can select COD and pay the delivery personnel when their water order arrives.

## Purpose

The main purpose of AquaFlow is to provide a convenient and organized way for customers to order water and schedule deliveries. It aims to simplify the traditional water ordering process by bringing product selection, cart management, delivery scheduling, and payment options into one system.
